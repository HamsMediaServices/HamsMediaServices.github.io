Browse Media Catalog

Smart RSS & Auto-Discovery

Add any RSS or Atom feed from across the open web. Simply type a source name or paste a website URL, and MEDIUS automatically discovers and pairs the exact feed address.

  • Intelligent feed detection by website name or URL
  • Full article reader with high-resolution imagery

Websites & Direct Links

Save your favorite publications, blogs, and niche websites directly as tiles on your dashboard. Launch into a clean, distraction-free reader view without browser clutter or open in your own browser.

  • Instant one-tap access to your essential web destinations
  • Seamlessly organized within your chosen category dashboard

Podcasts & Background Audio

Enjoy audio podcast episodes with continuous background playback. Keep listening while browsing other categories.

  • Persistent mini-player and full-screen playback controls
  • Quick skip (-15s / +15s) and automatic progress saving

Curated Video Channels

Track your favorite video creators and YouTube feeds in a clean, distraction-free grid. Tap any video tile to launch it directly and securely in the official YouTube app.

  • Organize YouTube feeds alongside your articles and podcasts
  • Direct handoff to the official YouTube app per Google guidelines

Offline PDF Documents

Store important PDF contracts, manuals, and documents right on your dashboard. Read, navigate, and inspect them anytime without relying on internet access or cloud sync.

  • High-performance PDF reader with smooth 4x pinch-to-zoom
  • 100% on-device offline storage with instant document opening

Photos & Images

Pin your favorite reference photos, inspirational pictures, and visual assets directly as dashboard tiles. Inspect details with full-screen zoom and complete local privacy.

  • Smooth full-resolution photo viewer with pan & zoom
  • Organized visual tiles directly in your custom categories

Personal Notes & Thoughts

Jot down quick thoughts, save article takeaways, or draft personal notes. Pin your notes directly as interactive dashboard tiles alongside your relevant topics.

  • Built-in note editor with direct tile integration
  • Easy sharing and instant access across your custom categories

Favorites & Bookmarks

Star articles, podcast episodes, and media items with a single tap. All your favorite discoveries are gathered into a dedicated, instant-access favorites hub.

  • One-tap starring across all media types
  • Dedicated favorites collection accessible anywhere in the app

Universal Searchable Archive

Search across all your RSS articles, personal notes, PDF documents, and feeds simultaneously. Instantly surface that one quote, article, or receipt with zero delay.

  • Search across titles, sources, categories, and note contents
  • Lightning-fast local query latency under 100 milliseconds

PIN Vault & Local Privacy

Your personal data belongs strictly to you. Lock sensitive categories behind a secure 4-digit PIN code. No tracking, no mandatory cloud sync.

  • Secure 4-digit PIN protection for confidential categories
  • 100% on-device encryption without external data mining

Dynamic Grid & Tile Layouts

Switch seamlessly between three distinct layout modes to suit your reading style. Whether you prefer a vibrant 3-column media grid, a focused single-column feed, or detailed cards, your dashboard adapts instantly with fluid drag-and-drop reordering.

  • 3 switchable viewing modes: 3-column grid, 1-column feed & detailed view
  • Fluid drag-and-drop tile reordering and 1-tap pinning to Home
